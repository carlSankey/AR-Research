# The staff of Acrinimiril (Dungeon)

Acrinimiril's staff is broken into **three pieces**. Return them to his tomb on level 1 to get the **Portal Access Card (PAC Card)**, which opens **Death's Door** on level 3.

## The pieces
1. **Ozob** – prisoner in the Palace prison, Dungeon level 1. You need the **Silver Key**, dropped by a **Master Thief**. Ozob gives you a piece and sends you to the Riddle of the Three Doors (see three-door-puzzle.md). Overlay 32-s126.
2. **King Danjor, the Undead King** – level 2, reached by the ferry across the River Stonz (at midnight, exactly 2 coppers – CRPG Addict), past the Seven Undead Knights. He calls you "The One" and gives you a piece. Overlay 22-s323.
3. **The Great Dragon** – level 3 lair. Overlay 22-s365.
   - Menu: offer a gift / attack / flee. Fleeing (or offering the wrong gift) gets you a parting blow from his tail or breath.
   - Offer the **Bloodstone** → he gives you "this stick" – the last piece.
   - Kill him instead → the lair can be searched: gold, silver, gems, jewels, coppers and the staff piece. Later searches find it picked clean (it can refill after a couple of days – **unverified**).
   - The **Bloodstone** is held by the **Basilisk** on level 3, which weapons can't hurt – fight it barehanded; the Mirrored Shield reflects its gaze (CRPG Addict).
   - The **Gargoyle** on level 3 asks three riddles whose answers are hints: SHIPWRECK (Xebec's Demise), ORACLE (where to get rid of the evil ring), BLOODSTONE (what the dragon wants). Overlay 22-s094.

## The tomb (level 1, 31,27) – overlay 32-s091
- First visit: Acrinimiril's ghost tells how the Masters destroyed his body, and asks you to find his broken staff.
- He takes **every** piece you carry, one at a time:
  - 1st piece → **+2 Intelligence**
  - 2nd piece → **+2 Wisdom**
  - 3rd piece → the **PAC Card**, then he fades away; afterwards the tomb is empty.
- No pieces: he tells you to seek the Oracle.
- (The stat amounts come from the ADDSTAT call: A = amount, as in other overlays.)

## Death's Door (level 3) – overlay 22-s409
- At the end of the Gauntlet. Menu: pass through / use an item; there is no turning back.
- Using the PAC Card there opens the way; passing through then takes you down to level 4.
- Without it you are teleported out of the Gauntlet.

## Not in the original fixed-treasure list
The PAC Card is not one of the Dungeon's fixed treasures (31-s115) – it only comes from the tomb.
