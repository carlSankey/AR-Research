# The Dungeon endgame

1. **Clothes Horse** (level 2) – a warhorse admiring himself in a room of mirrors. He wants your finest worn garment and trades his mirror for it: the **Mirrored Shield**. With only poor clothes he sends you away. Overlay 22-s168. (CRPG Addict traded a leather jacket; Morgana's Tiara also works.)
2. **Staff of Acrinimiril → PAC Card** – see staff-of-acrinimiril.md.
3. **The Gauntlet → Death's Door** (level 3) – fights inside the Gauntlet (fire demons, horned devil, small dragon, doppelganger – CRPG Addict), then Death's Door with the PAC Card. Overlay 22-s409.
4. **Level 4** – a small final level. You can't save there; if you die you come back "Lost" at the Dungeon entrance with a stat point less (**unverified** in detail).
5. **Alien Control Room** (level 4) – a guard fires his laser as you enter. A readied **Mirrored Shield** (checked in either hand) reflects the blast and kills him – you have solved one of the mysteries of Alternate Reality. Without it, the blast kills you. Overlay 22-s432.
6. **The lift / elevator** beyond the control room (CRPG Addict).
7. **Gateway to Revelation** on level 4 – leads on to the never-made *Revelation* scenario.

- A door was added at 1,2 (west side) so the arrival room isn't sealed.
